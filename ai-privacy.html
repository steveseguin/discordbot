NinjaBot AI Support Privacy Policy

Application ID: 1465030230107357275
Effective date: October 17, 2026

NinjaBot helps troubleshoot VDO.Ninja, Social Stream Ninja, and Raspberry Ninja in designated Discord support conversations. We use relevant conversation context to provide support and retain anonymized technical knowledge. We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or use Discord messages to train or fine-tune AI models.

1. Who operates this application

The operator is Skindeep.AI Inc., an Ontario corporation doing business as Ninjafy Inc. (“Operator,” “we,” or “us”). Steve Seguin is the service and privacy contact acting for the Operator. Project and bot names identify services; they do not identify additional legal entities or data controllers.

This policy covers the AI support application identified above, also known as StevesBot or NinjaClawd. The separate community-management bot, application 843169738556571680, has its own privacy policy. Discord controls its platform and the information it retains under Discord’s privacy policy.

2. Information used for support

In configured support channels and threads, NinjaBot processes relevant messages, replies, screenshots and other attachments, timestamps, message and thread links, and Discord identifiers and display names. This includes surrounding messages from participants who have not mentioned the bot. The application may identify unresolved support questions automatically, respond to a help request, or prepare a draft for the maintainer.

We also process support outcomes, feedback, limited interaction history, and operational information needed to run the service, prevent duplicate processing, diagnose faults, and handle privacy requests. Please do not post passwords, tokens, private keys, payment information, or sensitive personal information in support conversations.

3. Why the information is used

Conversation context helps establish the reported problem, software version, environment, attempted fixes, and outcome. NinjaBot combines that context with project documentation, source references, and relevant previous solutions. Technical summaries may describe a problem and its resolution so similar questions can be answered later. This is retrieval and inference, not model training.

4. Processing services and disclosures

Support processing uses infrastructure operated for the service and configured AI inference providers. Depending on the support route and provider availability, relevant context may be processed by the operator’s model service, OpenCode, Z.ai, or Kimi. Information is provided as needed to generate or review support responses. Providers may process information outside Canada and are subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

Support replies are posted in the relevant Discord conversation or made available to authorized maintainers. Raw support conversations are not published as a public dataset. We do not sell, license, or disclose personal information for advertising. We may disclose information when required by law, to respond to a valid legal request, or as reasonably necessary to address abuse, security incidents, or protect legal rights. These disclosures do not create an exception to the ordinary raw-message retention limit below.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Raw message content and downloaded attachments in storage controlled by the Operator are retained for no more than 30 days. Cleanup is scheduled before that deadline. Processing failures do not extend it.
  • The same limit applies to raw content copied into workflow records, diagnostic records, exports, and backups. Copying or re-exporting information does not restart its retention period.
  • Before deletion, useful support information may be converted into a separate, anonymized technical summary. Anonymized summaries may be retained while useful for support. They are not a substitute archive of identifiable conversations.
  • Minimal identifiers, processing markers, privacy preferences, and service records may remain while needed to operate the application or honor requests. These records do not preserve expired raw message content.
  • Deletion from the application’s storage does not delete the original messages from Discord or copies independently held by conversation participants.

6. Your choices and requests

Email steve@seguin.email to request access, correction, deletion, or an explanation of how your information is handled. Include your Discord user ID and relevant message or thread links so we can locate the information. Do not email passwords or authentication tokens. We may reasonably verify your identity before acting and will respond as required by applicable law.

Avoiding a bot mention or the Ask the Bot button does not by itself exclude messages posted in a configured support conversation. Contact the privacy contact if you do not want your information processed. Server administrators can restrict the channels accessible to the bot or remove it. Once information has been irreversibly anonymized, it may no longer be possible to associate a technical summary with a particular person.

7. Security and responsibilities

The support service stores its working databases, downloaded attachments, and runtime logs in encrypted storage. Recovery keys are kept separately from the support server's persistent storage. Access to decrypted information is limited to the running service and authorized administration.

We use access restrictions, limited retention, and administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access and use. Access is limited to people and systems that need it for support operations. Security incidents are handled and reported as required by applicable law and platform obligations.

No service can eliminate every security risk. This policy describes the Operator’s data practices; it is not a personal guarantee by Steve Seguin or a promise of uninterrupted service, error-free AI output, or absolute security. Nothing in this policy excludes mandatory privacy rights, releases responsibility that cannot lawfully be excluded, or overrides applicable law or Discord’s developer terms.

8. Changes and contact

We update this policy when our data practices change and identify the effective date above. Material changes will be communicated where required. Privacy questions and complaints should be directed to Steve Seguin, the privacy contact for Skindeep.AI Inc., doing business as Ninjafy Inc., at steve@seguin.email.
