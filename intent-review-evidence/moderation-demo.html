The two spam checks

  1. Attachment/image-link rate: for each author in each channel, count messages containing attachments or supported image links during the preceding 30 seconds. Delete messages beyond five and warn on the sixth. Text-only messages without supported image links do not increment this counter. Each qualifying message counts once, regardless of its number of attachments.
  2. Repeated text across channels: compare a person's new message with their previous message. When their recent activity spans multiple channels, identical or almost identical text increases a spam score. Repeated violations lead to message removal and a kick. This catches repeated text even when it contains no banned words. Bots and trusted staff/sponsor roles are excluded.

Read the text-comparison and attachment-counter implementation. The separate three-channel activity rule uses channel IDs and does not itself need Message Content.

What stops working without Message Content?

Discord sends ordinary message events with empty text and attachment lists. NinjaBot can tell that someone posted, but cannot tell what they wrote or whether they attached a file.

  • No text to compare: the bot cannot distinguish repeated spam from different, legitimate messages.
  • No way to identify attachment or image-link messages: the bot cannot tell which messages belong in the 30-second count. Counting every message instead would also penalize normal text conversations.

Why commands and AutoMod do not replace these checks

AlternativeWhat the custom checks require
Slash commands, mentions, DMs, and message commandsThese tools require someone to send or select a message for the bot. People posting spam do not have to do that, so these tools cannot replace automatic checks on ordinary messages.
Keyword and regex rulesA repeated message can contain no banned words. NinjaBot compares what a person posted with what they posted before.
Generic spam and mention-spam rulesDiscord's documented settings do not let us configure these comparisons between a person's recent messages or this combined attachment/image-link limit over 30 seconds.

Discord documents the relevant message-content fields and alternatives and AutoMod triggers and configuration.

Limited moderation data

Moderation checks use temporary memory, not a saved chat archive. New text replaces the previous text being compared. Every two minutes, the bot clears records for people inactive for more than 60 seconds. Staff moderation reports remain inside Discord; the management bot does not keep a message-history archive or use these messages for AI responses or model training.
